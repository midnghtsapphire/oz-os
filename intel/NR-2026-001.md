# NULL_RESULT: No deterministic method recovers occluded facial geometry

## Summary

The pack was commissioned to find deterministic, open-source methods that
"triage and heal missing geometry using math" behind a facial covering. No such
method exists, and none can. This is a `evidence_contradicts_premise` null:
the search did not fail to find the answer, it established that the question is
malformed.

## Adjacent Searches

After the direct queries returned only generative methods, the search broadened
to: what *is* deterministically recoverable under occlusion; whether any
sampling-theory result covers coherent structured loss; and whether multimodal
retrieval agents close the gap. All three produced usable results, which became
methods 1–10 of the pack.

## Time Spent

Approximately one full research cycle, concurrent with implementation.

## Reason for Null — the argument

Model the observation as `y = Ax + n`.

- A **blur or downsample** operator attenuates without annihilating. Regularised
  inversion recovers `x` to the noise floor with a computable bound. This is
  Case 1 and it is genuinely "healing with math".
- A **mask** is a projection. It has a non-trivial null space `ker(A)`, and for
  any `v ∈ ker(A)`, `A(x̂ + v) = Ax̂` — the measurement is identical. The data
  constrains nothing there. Non-uniqueness is exact, not approximate.
- **Compressed sensing** recovers null-space content only under sparsity *and*
  RIP. A contiguous physical occluder produces coherent, localised, structured
  loss — the opposite of the incoherent sampling CS requires. Invoking CS here
  cites a theorem outside its hypotheses.

Therefore any system that outputs facial geometry behind a mask is sampling a
prior, and its output resembles the training population rather than the subject.
It has no per-case error rate, which is also why it fails *Daubert*.

## What Was Found Instead — the one exception

If the occluder **moves relative to the scene** across frames, different frames
annihilate different subspaces and the union of observations may cover the
whole. Recovery is then deterministic, with per-pixel provenance. This is
testable in advance by counting per-pixel observations across registered frames,
and it is implemented as the mandatory gate in
`research-packs/occlusion-forensics/reference-impl/occlusion_forensics/coverage.py`.

Note the practical trap this immediately exposed: multiple screenshots or
re-renders of a *single* video frame are one observation, not several.
Registration reports `dy = dx = 0.000` across such a set and coverage does not
close. Independence must be measured, never assumed from file count.

## Value of This Null

Knowing this does not exist prevents future agents from re-running the search,
and — more usefully — redirects the effort. The measurement half of the original
request was entirely buildable and shipped as a tested implementation. On the
motivating case, that toolset produced the decisive finding the reconstruction
never could have: the supplied "enhanced" frames were one source frame
re-rendered, and the final frame contained drawn detail absent from the original.
